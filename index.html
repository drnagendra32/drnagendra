Our Core Philosophy

We believe dental care should be defined by transparency and empathy, not just clinical procedures.

We Listen Before We Treat

Every patient has a unique story. We take the time to understand your concerns, symptoms, and expectations before recommending any treatment.

We Explain Everything Clearly

No confusing dental terms. We'll help you understand what's happening, why it's happening, and the best ways to treat it—using simple, easy-to-understand language.

Digital X-Rays Only When Needed

We recommend digital X-rays only when they're clinically necessary to make an accurate diagnosis and create the right treatment plan.

You Decide—With Complete Info

Before any major treatment begins, we'll explain all suitable options, expected outcomes, and costs so you can make an informed decision with confidence.

No Pressure. No Unnecessary Treatment.

Our recommendations are based on what's best for your oral health—not on selling treatments. If a tooth can be saved, we'll always try to save it.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What are your clinic hours?

Our clinic is open Monday through Saturday from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM. We are also open on Sundays from 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM. Emergency services are available on call.

Do I need to book an appointment beforehand?

While we do accept walk-in patients, we highly recommend booking an appointment in advance. This allows us to minimize your waiting time and dedicate ample time to your consultation and treatment.

Are dental treatments painful?

At Divya Dental Clinic, your comfort is our top priority. We use modern, minimally invasive techniques and highly effective local anesthesia to ensure that most procedures, including root canals and extractions, are virtually painless.

What types of dental implants do you offer?

We use premium, biocompatible titanium implants that integrate perfectly with your jawbone. We offer single tooth implants, multiple implants, and full mouth rehabilitation depending on your specific needs.

How long does a root canal procedure take?

With our advanced rotary endodontics and high-magnification loupes, most root canal treatments can be completed efficiently in a single visit, typically taking between 45 to 60 minutes.

Is there an initial consultation fee?

Yes, our standard initial consultation fee is approximately ₹100. During this visit, Dr. Nagendra Chauhan will conduct a thorough examination, take time to listen to your concerns, and provide a clear, detailed treatment plan.
